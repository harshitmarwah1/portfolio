Self-directed case study · B2B SaaS Prototype live

Vendor onboarding, in one workflow

vendor-onboarding-mvp.vercel.app/admin

In most companies, onboarding a vendor takes fifteen to thirty business days and runs across email, spreadsheets, and four separate systems. Nobody can see where a vendor is stuck.

Role
Product manager and builder. I framed the problem, scoped the MVP, and built the working prototype.
What I built
A self-service onboarding portal with role-based access and department SLAs. A clearance model that runs Legal, Financial, Personnel, and Security checks in parallel, with the whole vendor lifecycle modeled as a state machine.
Outcome
Designed to move onboarding from a fifteen-to-thirty-day scramble to one tracked workflow every team can see.

Someone shows up when it matters

saath-pitch.html

Self-directed case study · Logistics Prototype built

The last 200 metres nobody owns

Contribution margin per society. Payback drops from 5.3 months to 0.4.

India runs 7.8 million quick-commerce orders a day. The last stretch, from the society gate to the door, lands on security desks and riders. One 600-flat society pushes more than 280 parcels a day through a single desk.

Role
Product and business modeling. I built the operating model and the case for it.
What I built
A micro-logistics hub at the gate: smart lockers, verified runners, and a fifteen-minute delivery circuit. I modeled the unit economics and a seed-stage business case around it.
Outcome
Unit economics that move from 27% margin in month one to 48% by month twenty-four, with payback inside a few months.
  • ₹2,900 CrTotal market
  • 27→48%Margin, mo. 1 to 24
  • 7.8MOrders a day
